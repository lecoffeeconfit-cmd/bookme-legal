Subscriptions, Billing & Purchases

BookMe may offer auto-renewable subscription plans. The exact plan name, duration, price, and any trial or introductory offer are shown inside the app and in Apple's purchase confirmation sheet before you subscribe.

How do I cancel my BookMe subscription?

Apple manages subscriptions purchased through the App Store. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BookMe, then choose the available cancellation option. Cancel before the next renewal date if you do not want the subscription to renew.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open BookMe while signed into the Apple Account that originally purchased the subscription and use Restore Purchases on the subscription/paywall screen where available.

I paid, but premium features are still locked.

Confirm that you are using the same Apple Account that made the purchase, check that the subscription is active in Apple Subscriptions, reopen BookMe, and try Restore Purchases. If access still does not return, email lecoffeeconfit@gmail.com.

How do refunds work?

App Store purchases are billed by Apple. Refund eligibility and refund requests for App Store transactions are handled under Apple's applicable purchase and refund policies.

Important: deleting the BookMe app or deleting a BookMe account does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription. Subscription cancellation is managed separately through Apple.

Account Help & Account Deletion

How do I delete my BookMe account?

If you created a BookMe account, use the Delete Account option available inside BookMe's account/settings area. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app to initiate deletion of your account and associated personal data, except information that may need to be retained for legitimate legal, fraud-prevention, security, or transaction-record purposes.

If you cannot access your account or the deletion control is not working, contact lecoffeeconfit@gmail.com and write BookMe Account Deletion Request in the subject line.

I can't sign in.

Double-check the email/account method you originally used, verify your internet connection, and retry. If the issue continues, email support and describe the sign-in method and any error message you see.

Can I delete individual writing without deleting my account?

Where BookMe provides deletion controls for books, chapters, images, or other content, you can use those controls directly. Account deletion is separate and removes the account itself subject to applicable retention requirements.

Community, Reports & Blocking

BookMe may allow users to share writing, book information, profile images, reactions, and other user-generated content.

How do I report inappropriate content or a user?

Use the available Report control associated with the content or user inside BookMe. Reports may be reviewed for violations of BookMe's community rules and Terms of Service.

How do I block an abusive user?

Use BookMe's available Block control from the relevant user/profile interface. If you cannot access the control or need additional help, contact support.

What content is not allowed?

BookMe does not permit unlawful, exploitative, threatening, harassing, fraudulent, malicious, or rights-infringing content, including illegal sexual content involving minors, credible threats, malware, scams, or other content prohibited by the Terms of Service.

AI-Assisted Writing Support

BookMe may offer optional AI-assisted features such as brainstorming, rewriting, polishing, expanding, shortening, grammar assistance, and contextual writing suggestions.

Why can AI output be different each time?

AI-generated responses are probabilistic and may vary even when prompts are similar. Review and edit AI output before relying on or publishing it.

What information can be processed when I use an AI feature?

When you intentionally activate an AI tool, the prompt, selected writing, and contextual information needed to complete the request may be processed as described in the BookMe Privacy Policy and any applicable in-app notice or consent flow.

Is AI required to use BookMe?

No. BookMe's core writing experience can be used independently of optional AI-assisted tools.

Troubleshooting

If something isn't working correctly, try these steps:

  • Confirm your device has a stable internet connection.
  • Close and reopen BookMe.
  • Confirm you are signed into the intended BookMe account.
  • Install the latest available version of BookMe from the App Store.
  • Restart your iPhone or iPad if the issue continues.
  • For subscription-access problems, try Restore Purchases.
  • For persistent problems, contact support with the device model, iOS version, app version/build, and a description of what happened.
If BookMe crashes repeatedly or you believe your data is at risk, stop repeating the action that triggers the issue and contact support with as much non-sensitive detail as possible.
